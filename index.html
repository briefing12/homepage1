About the area

A busy part of Kigali with a story to tell.

Located in Kigali, Nyabugogo brings together commuters, travellers, traders and service providers. Its transport links make it an important starting point for many journeys across Rwanda.

The area is also shaped by everyday commerce: shops, food vendors, small businesses and services that support the people passing through and living nearby.

  • A key transport connection point
  • A lively trading and business area
  • A meeting point for people from different places

Plan your visit

Useful tips before you go

A little preparation can make your trip through a busy transport and business area easier.

Confirm your route

Ask the transport operator or station staff about your destination, departure point and latest schedule.

Keep belongings secure

As in any busy public place, keep your phone, wallet and bags close, especially in crowded areas.

Allow extra time

Traffic and passenger activity can vary. Leave some extra time when travelling for an appointment.
